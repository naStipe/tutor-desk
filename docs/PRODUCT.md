# TutorDesk Product Overview

## What is TutorDesk?

TutorDesk is a focused business management SaaS built specifically for independent private tutors.

**Value Proposition:** "Run your tutoring business from one place."

TutorDesk consolidates the operational and administrative side of running a solo tutoring practice, eliminating the fragmented toolchain tutors currently deal with daily.

---

## User Roles

### Primary User
* **The Tutor**: An independent, self-employed educator managing their own student roster, scheduling, lesson records, homework assignments, and invoicing.

### Secondary User
* **The Student** (and/or their parents/guardians): Receives lesson schedule information, completes text homework assignments, views payment status and invoices via a lightweight, focused student portal.

---

## What TutorDesk is NOT

To maintain product focus and operational simplicity, TutorDesk is explicitly NOT:

1. **A Tutor Marketplace**: TutorDesk does not find students for tutors, rank tutors in search algorithms, or take commission fees on tutor earnings. Tutors bring and manage their own client relationships.
2. **A School Management Platform**: TutorDesk is not an enterprise SIS/LMS built for institutional administrators, complex grade books, multi-teacher departments, or hierarchical school districts.
3. **A Video-Conferencing Product**: TutorDesk does not build or host real-time video/audio streaming infrastructure. Tutors use their preferred meeting links (Google Meet, Zoom, etc.) or teach in person.

---

## The Core Problem

Today, independent tutors run their businesses by cobbling together disconnected tools:
* **Calendars** (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar) for scheduling.
* **Spreadsheets** (Excel, Google Sheets) for tracking hours, lesson logs, and student information.
* **Messaging Apps** (WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram) for lesson confirmations, homework reminders, and updates.
* **File Sharing / Notes** (Google Drive, Notion, email threads) for assigning tasks.
* **Manual Payment Reminders** via banking apps or manual emails to chase unpaid invoices.

This fragmentation leads to:
* Administrative fatigue and lost billable hours.
* Missed or forgotten lesson charges and late payments.
* Poor client communication with scattered assignment trails.
* Inability to scale or professionally organize a growing student base.

---

## Long-Term Vision

TutorDesk provides a cohesive, single-workspace solution for independent educators:
* **Student Directory & Profiles**: Clean profiles tracking subject, level, hourly rate, contact info, and notes.
* **Tutor-Owned Calendar & Lessons**: Flexible recurring schedules, single sessions, cancellations, and status tracking.
* **Structured Homework**: Plain text assignments, student submissions, and tutor feedback.
* **Billing & Invoicing**: Automated invoice generation from completed lessons, custom due dates, scheduled reminders, and clear payment tracking.
* **Student Portals**: Dedicated read/respond views for students and parents without requiring complex account onboarding.
